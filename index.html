Why corporate-only

Our largest revenue channel is and will continue to be employer-paid. The vast majority of our revenue growth comes from corporate sales. Most product-led companies have a singular audience and message — and the data shows we lose a meaningful portion of visitors at the homepage because we're not focused enough on what we're really selling.

What changes

No "for personal / for business / for intermediaries" tabs. No three-way navigation tax. This is Simplyhealth, this is what it does, this is who it's for. A focused message aimed squarely at corporates buying healthcare for their employees. Brokers and intermediaries become a distribution channel, not a separate audience.

Step 3 of 3 · Employee experience

One app. The same brand. Different access.

Whichever plan the employer picks, the employee opens the same app. Features unlock or lock based on the plan — but the employee always sees what's possible. This is how Spotify, Notion and every successful SaaS handles tiering: you make the upgrade obvious without making the current state feel cheap.

What this solves

Today, an employee on Essentials and an employee on Optimise have different apps and different experiences — which means different brand impressions, different support journeys, and zero cross-pollination of upgrade demand. One unified experience fixes all three.

The upgrade ladder

An On-Demand employee sees self-pay prices on every service. An Essentials employee sees those same services as "Included." An Optimise employee additionally sees their claim-back pots filling up. Each tier feels like a real upgrade — and creates natural internal demand for the next.
